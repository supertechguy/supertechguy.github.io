About

Hi, I’m Jeremy Cox.

I’m the engineer behind SUPERTECHGUY, LLC, based in St. George, Utah.

I’ve spent more than 20 years working in technology, with a focus on enterprise networking and information security. I started SUPERTECHGUY to give organizations in Southern Utah direct access to that experience — the same person who scopes the work designs it, builds it, and trains your team on it.

I’m passionate about IT security and about sharing what I know. I serve as Co-Chair of the SAINTCON committee and on the UtahSAINT Board of Trustees, and I’ve presented dozens of sessions at SAINTCON and the UETN Tech Summit.

When I’m offline, you’ll find me in the mountains and canyons with my family. I’m also a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
